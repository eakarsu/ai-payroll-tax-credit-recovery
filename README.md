# Payroll Tax Credit Recovery

Identify payroll-based credits, calculate eligible wages, and reconcile amended filings and refunds.

**Primary buyer:** Employers and payroll providers. **Evidence:** employee wages, work locations, tax deposits, qualified populations, state and federal credits, amendments, notices, and refunds.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Employer location registry
- Employee wage ingestion
- Eligibility population matching
- Certification deadline control
- Qualified wage calculation
- Credit interaction rules
- Federal credit calculation
- State credit calculation
- Payroll deposit reconciliation
- Form amendment preparation
- Supporting document package
- Tax notice workflow
- Refund tracking
- General-ledger reconciliation
- Program location analytics
